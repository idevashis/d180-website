THE STAGE

16-minute talks.

D180 talks are built around a 16-minute format. The idea is to give one meaningful idea or story enough space to be explored without losing the energy of the room.

01 A perspective
02 A story
03 An idea
04 A discovery
05 A question worth exploring
WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR

You don't need to be famous.

You could be a student, entrepreneur, artist, researcher, leader, creator, educator or simply someone who has lived through something that changed the way you see the world.

You need to have something worth saying.
