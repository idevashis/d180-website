16MINUTE TALKS
THE FORMAT

16 minutes can be enough to make you look again.

D180 talks are built around a 16-minute format. Long enough to explore an idea with depth. Short enough to keep the room moving.

The focus is not on performance for performance's sake. It is on giving an idea, experience or discovery enough room to breathe.

THE ROOM

One room.
Many perspectives.

D180 brings together people from different walks of life · students, professionals, founders, creators, educators and others who are curious about the world around them.

The room matters as much as the stage. The people you meet, the questions you hear and the conversations that happen between talks are part of the experience.
