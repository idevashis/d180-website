Privacy Policy

D180 ("D180", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you when you interact with the D180 website, forms, events, programs, and related services.

By using the D180 website or submitting information through any D180 form, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. About D180

D180 is a platform built around discovery, ideas, experiences, perspectives, and meaningful conversations.

Our website may allow individuals to:

  • Learn about D180 and its initiatives
  • Apply to become a speaker
  • Register or express interest in attending D180 events
  • Submit partnership or sponsorship enquiries
  • Contact the D180 team
  • Receive updates about upcoming events and activities
  • Interact with D180 through third-party platforms and social media

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Organisation or institution
  • Job title or professional information
  • City or location
  • Speaker information and biography
  • Areas of expertise or interest
  • Social media or professional profile links
  • Partnership or sponsorship information
  • Event registration information
  • Any information you voluntarily include in forms, applications, messages, or enquiries

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically requested and necessary for the relevant D180 activity.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the services and technologies we use. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location information
  • Referring website
  • Date and time of visits
  • Website interaction and usage information

This information may be collected through cookies, analytics tools, hosting services, or similar technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process speaker applications
  • Review and respond to partnership enquiries
  • Manage audience registrations and event participation
  • Communicate with speakers, partners, attendees, and applicants
  • Send event-related announcements and updates
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Understand website usage and engagement
  • Organise and administer D180 events
  • Maintain records related to applications and participation
  • Protect the security and integrity of our website and services
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Where applicable, we may also use your information to send promotional or informational communications about D180, subject to applicable law and your preferences.

4. Event Photography and Videography

D180 events may be photographed, recorded, or filmed for documentation, promotional, educational, archival, and marketing purposes.

By attending a D180 event, you may appear in photographs, video recordings, or other event-related content.

Where required, D180 will provide appropriate notice or obtain consent in accordance with applicable requirements.

If you have a specific concern regarding your appearance in D180 content, you may contact us using the contact information provided on this website.

5. How We Share Information

D180 does not sell your personal information.

We may share relevant information with trusted service providers, contractors, technology providers, event partners, or other parties where reasonably necessary to operate our website, organise events, process applications, communicate with you, or provide services.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, governmental authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of D180 or others.

We aim to share only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Third-Party Services

D180 may use third-party services such as:

  • Online form providers
  • Website hosting providers
  • Analytics services
  • Communication and email platforms
  • Social media platforms
  • Payment providers, where applicable
  • Event management or registration platforms

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The D180 website may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Keep the website functioning properly
  • Understand website traffic
  • Improve website performance
  • Remember certain preferences
  • Measure engagement

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Certain website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate records, to provide services, to resolve disputes, or to comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to:

  • Request information about how your personal data is processed
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Opt out of certain communications
  • Raise concerns regarding the processing of your personal information

Requests may be submitted through our contact details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

11. Children's Privacy

D180 does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

Where an event or initiative is specifically designed for participants under the applicable age of consent, appropriate processes and permissions may be implemented.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

D180 may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our activities, technology, legal requirements, or practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact D180 through the contact information provided on our website.
