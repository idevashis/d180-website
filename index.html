04 — WHY I STARTED D180
DEVASHIS JENAFOUNDER · D180
A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

What if there was
more to see?

I believe most of us spend our lives building perspectives.

We learn from our experiences, form opinions, discover what we believe to be true, and over time, those perspectives become the way we understand the world.

But there is something we often forget: what we know may not be the whole picture.

A truth we have discovered may only be part of a larger truth. A perspective that feels complete may be missing another point of view. A lesson we have learned may be true for us, but not necessarily the entire story.

05 — MISSION & VISION
OUR MISSION

To spark curiosity, uncover meaningful discoveries and create moments that change perspective.

D180 brings together people from different walks of life to share ideas, insights, experiences, knowledge and perspectives that invite us to look beyond the familiar. Through every conversation, we seek to help people question what they know, discover what they haven't considered and see the world in a new way.

OUR VISION

A world where discovery continually expands how we understand ourselves and everything around us.

We aspire to make curiosity a lifelong habit and new perspectives a catalyst for deeper understanding, better questions and meaningful change. We envision a world where people remain open to discovering what they don't know and willing to see beyond their own point of view.

07 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions,
answered.

What exactly is D180?

D180 is a platform built around discovery and perspective. The D stands for Discover. 180 represents a shift in perspective. D180 brings together people from different walks of life to share ideas, insights, experiences, knowledge and perspectives that invite us to look beyond the familiar. It is a place where you can bring what you know, question what you believe, discover what you haven't considered and leave seeing something a little differently. D180 — See the world differently.

Who can attend D180?

D180 is for anyone who is curious about ideas, experiences, discoveries and different perspectives. Students, professionals, founders, creators, educators and people from different walks of life are welcome.

When and where is D180 happening?

The D180 First Edition is happening on 10 October 2026 in Hyderabad. The final venue details will be shared with registered attendees.

How do I get my D180 pass?

Click GET YOUR PASS on the D180 website and complete the audience registration process. Once your registration is received, the D180 team will share the next steps with you.

What can I expect at D180?

D180 is built around conversations, stories, ideas, discoveries and perspectives. You can expect 16-minute talks, a carefully curated audience and a room designed to encourage curiosity, listening and meaningful conversations.

Do I need to know the speakers before attending?

No. D180 is not built only around familiar names. The focus is on what is being discovered, shared and questioned. You may walk into the room knowing very little about a speaker and leave thinking differently because of something they shared.

Can I attend with my friends or colleagues?

Yes. You can attend with friends, colleagues, classmates or members of your community, subject to pass availability.

What should I wear?

The dress code for D180 attendees is Smart Formals, Formals or Semi-Formals. We encourage you to dress in a way that feels polished, comfortable and appropriate for the D180 experience.

Will there be networking?

Yes. D180 is designed not only around the stage, but also around the people in the room. There will be opportunities to meet and interact with people from different backgrounds, professions and walks of life.

Will I receive a certificate for attending?

Yes. Every registered attendee who attends D180 will receive a certificate of participation.

Who can apply to speak at D180?

Anyone with something worth sharing can apply. You could have a perspective, story, idea, discovery or question that deserves to be explored. You don't need to be famous. You need to have something worth saying.

How long is a D180 talk?

Each D180 talk is designed around a 16-minute format. The idea is to give one meaningful idea or story enough space to be explored without losing the energy of the room.

What kind of topics are you looking for?

D180 is intentionally broad. We are looking for ideas, discoveries, experiences and perspectives from different fields and walks of life. The question is less “What is your profession?” and more: “What have you discovered that others might benefit from seeing?”

Do I need to be an experienced speaker?

No. You don't need to be a professional speaker or someone who has spoken on large stages before. What matters is whether you have something meaningful to share and can communicate it honestly and clearly.

Do I need to be famous or well-known?

No. D180 is not about celebrity or popularity. It is about bringing different people and different ways of seeing into the same room.

Can I talk about my business, company or work?

Your work can absolutely be part of your story. However, D180 talks are not intended to be sales pitches or advertisements. The audience should leave with an idea, discovery, experience or perspective — not simply a promotion.

Can I apply if I am a student?

Yes. A meaningful discovery does not depend on age or designation. If you have an idea, experience, perspective or question that you believe is worth sharing, you can apply.

Does applying guarantee that I will be selected?

No. Submitting an application does not guarantee a speaking opportunity. The D180 team will review applications and contact selected speakers regarding the next steps.

Can I apply more than once?

No. Please submit one application per person. Make sure your application represents the idea, story, perspective or discovery you genuinely want to bring to the D180 stage.

What happens after I apply?

The D180 team will review your submission. If your proposal is shortlisted, the team will contact you regarding the next stage, including refining the idea and preparing the talk.

What is the dress code for speakers?

The dress code for D180 speakers is Smart Formals, Formals or Semi-Formals. We encourage speakers to choose an outfit that feels polished, comfortable and reflects the professional nature of the D180 stage.

Who can become a D180 partner?

Organisations, companies, brands, institutions and communities that want to contribute to the D180 experience can explore partnership opportunities.

What types of partnerships are available?

D180 can explore different forms of partnership, including Title Partner, Knowledge Partner, Production & Technology Partner, Venue Partner, Hospitality Partner, Travel Partner, Campus Partner, Media Partner, Community Partner, Wellness Partner and Strategic Partner. The final structure can be discussed based on the nature of the partnership.

What does a D180 partner receive?

Depending on the partnership, opportunities may include brand visibility, stage recognition, audience engagement, content opportunities, networking and association with the D180 experience. Specific benefits will depend on the partnership structure.

Can we create a customised partnership?

Yes. D180 is open to exploring partnerships built around the partner's objectives, capabilities and the kind of experience they want to help create.

Does a company have to become the Title Partner?

No. There are multiple ways to contribute to D180 beyond a title partnership. An organisation can support D180 through knowledge, technology, production, venue, media, community, hospitality or other strategic contributions.

Can we become a partner without providing financial sponsorship?

Yes. D180 welcomes both financial and non-financial partnerships where an organisation can contribute meaningful resources, expertise, reach, infrastructure or capabilities.

How do we become a D180 partner?

Complete the BECOME A PARTNER form on the website. The D180 team will review your enquiry and connect with you to understand what you would like to build with D180.

Can media organisations or communities partner with D180?

Yes. D180 can explore media and community partnerships that help bring the conversations and discoveries beyond the physical room.

Can we partner with D180 for future editions as well?

Yes. D180 is being built as a long-term platform, and partnerships can potentially extend beyond the First Edition. Existing partners will have the first right to renew their partnership for future editions before the opportunity is opened to others.

What is the dress code for D180 partners?

The dress code for D180 partners is Smart Formals, Formals or Semi-Formals. We encourage partners and representatives attending the event to dress in a polished and professional manner appropriate for the D180 experience.
