What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember certain information and understand how visitors interact with the site.

How D180 May Use Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to:

  • Keep the website functioning properly
  • Understand website traffic
  • Analyse website performance
  • Remember preferences
  • Improve website experience
  • Measure engagement with D180 content

Third-Party Technologies

Some third-party services integrated into the D180 website may use their own cookies or similar technologies. These may include analytics, embedded media, social media, hosting, or other technology providers.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Updates

D180 may update this Cookie Policy when our website, technology, or legal requirements change.
